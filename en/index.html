    Questions

    What to know before you come

    Tap a strip; the answer opens sideways. On health questions your doctor always has the final word.

    01pregnant_womanI am pregnant; can I come?

    Ask your doctor first. With their approval we suggest a lukewarm foam and a facial instead of the hot stone and salt room, and keep the session short.

    02favoriteI have high blood pressure

    Hot areas may not suit everyone; ask your doctor before booking and mention it on the form. The therapist sets the temperature with you.

    03checkroomWhat should I bring?

    Nothing; peshtemal, towel, slippers and single-use underwear are on us. We suggest leaving jewellery at home.

    04event_busyCan I cancel?

    Just message us on WhatsApp up to 12 hours before the session; there is no charge (fictional terms).

    05scheduleHow early should I arrive?

    Come 15 minutes early to fill in the short health form and have your salted lemonade.
